# Future Interns — Task 1: Business Sales Performance Analytics

## Internship
Future Interns — Business Analytics Internship

## Task
Task 1: Business Sales Performance Analytics

## Project Overview
This project analyzes Superstore sales data to understand business performance across sales, profit, product categories, regions, discount levels, and individual products.

The objective is to transform raw transactional data into business insights and recommendations using Power BI.

## Dataset
**Dataset:** Sample - Superstore

The dataset contains transactional information including:
- Order and shipping dates
- Customer and product information
- Sales
- Quantity
- Discount
- Profit
- Category
- Region

## Tools Used
- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- GitHub

## Key KPIs
- Total Sales: $2.30M
- Total Profit: $286.40K
- Total Orders: 5,009
- Total Quantity: ~38K
- Profit Margin: 12.47%

## Analysis Performed

### 1. Sales Performance
Analyzed sales trends across 2014–2017 to understand overall business performance.

### 2. Category Performance
Compared Sales, Profit, and Profit Margin across:
- Technology
- Office Supplies
- Furniture

### 3. Regional Performance
Analyzed business performance across:
- West
- East
- Central
- South

### 4. Discount Analysis
Created discount bands to examine the relationship between discount levels and profitability.

### 5. Product Analysis
Identified:
- Top products by sales
- Loss-making products
- High-revenue products with negative profit

## Key Business Insights

- Total sales were approximately $2.30M with $286.40K profit and a 12.47% profit margin.
- Technology and Office Supplies showed substantially higher margins than Furniture.
- West generated the highest regional profit, while Central had the lowest profit margin.
- Higher discount levels were strongly associated with lower profitability in the dataset.
- Several products generated significant sales while still producing negative profit.

## Recommendations

- Review heavily discounted transactions, particularly discounts above 20%.
- Investigate Furniture profitability through product, pricing, discount, and cost analysis.
- Investigate the Central region's lower profitability.
- Review consistently loss-making products before making pricing or product-mix decisions.
- Monitor Sales, Profit, and Profit Margin together rather than evaluating revenue alone.

## Dashboard

- Business insights and recommendations

The project demonstrates the use of business analytics techniques to convert transactional sales data into actionable business insights using Power BI.
